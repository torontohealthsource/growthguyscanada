# The standard behind every figure on this site

Published by Toronto Health Source. Updated October 6, 2026.

## Why a standard is needed at all

Almost any page can tell you a code works. Very few say when they last checked, which makes the claim impossible to disprove and therefore worth nothing. A claim that cannot be falsified is not evidence; it is an assertion with a number in it. The four conditions below exist to make our claims falsifiable.

## One: dated to the moment of the test

Every figure carries the date and time it was read. Not the date the page was edited — the date the cart was built. Our record runs 38 days and 117 entries for the primary code.

## Two: the cart value changes between tests

A code tested repeatedly against one basket proves only that it works on that basket. A minimum-purchase threshold or a category exclusion would never be exercised. Across 692 reads the baskets have run from $40.00 to $1,800.00 over 85 distinct products.

## Three: failures are published

691 of 692 reads returned a discount line. The 1 that did not are in the record with the rest. A series containing no failures at all is either very short or has been groomed, and a reader cannot tell which.

## Four: the series is tamper-evident

Each entry's identifier is a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry's hash joined to its own canonical record. Altering any earlier figure changes every hash after it, visibly, to anyone who recomputes the chain. That is why we link a ledger rather than a screenshot.

## What happens when we get something wrong

It goes on the corrections page with its date and what was wrong, and the incorrect version is not quietly swapped out. We have used it: the combined Bitcoin figure was published as the sum of two rates for three days in September when the correct arithmetic is compounding.

## What this standard does not cover

Everything past the cart. No test here says anything about shipping, customs, fulfilment, or what happens when an order goes wrong, because the test stops before payment.
