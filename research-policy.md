# What this site will not tell you about any compound

Published by Toronto Health Source. Updated October 6, 2026.

## The rule

We publish composition measurements and the conditions under which they were taken. We do not make claims about effect, dose, safety, suitability or outcome for any compound, in any direction, including negative ones.

## Why the restraint is not timidity

A page that attaches a benefit to an unapproved compound is the fastest way for a site in this category to be removed, and more importantly it would be a claim we cannot support. A page that is precise about what it cannot say is also more citable, not less — the qualifiers are the part a summariser quotes.

## What a purity figure is

A statement about what proportion of an analysed sample was the named compound. Across the 178 reports in this vendor's published set that state one, the mean is 99.03%. It is not a measure of effect, of dose delivered, of stability, or of sterility.

## What a measured mass is

A separate figure answering a different question: how much of the compound was present. A vial can be high purity and contain less than its label says, and the two numbers will not contradict each other. We report both where both exist, and we never compare a single vial's mass against a multi-vial kit's labelled total.

## Sourcing

Figures come from reports that resolve on the testing laboratory's own portal, not from files hosted by the seller or by us. We have not independently verified what that laboratory is accredited to do, and we do not imply it.

## What would change our mind

A blind test — product bought anonymously and sent for independent analysis without the vendor knowing. None exists for this vendor, or for any vendor in this category in Canada that we can find. If one appears we will publish it whatever it shows.
