# Corrections
Every correction we have made to a published figure, with the date and what changed. Nothing is removed from this list.

## 2026-10-04 — the combined Bitcoin figure read 30%, and was 28%

What was wrong. Pages and posts stated that paying with Bitcoin and applying the
code together took 30% off. Two percentage reductions do not add: the code comes off the
subtotal first and the payment reduction applies to what is left, which compounds to 28%.
On a $365.50 cart the real saving was $102.34, not the $109.65 that adding the two
percentages implies — an overstatement of $7.31.

How long it stood. Three days, 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-03.

What changed. Every page and template now derives the figure by compounding, and
the published cart lines are read from a single cart so that they sum to the total. An
article that argued the additive case has been rewritten.

## 2026-10-05 — the assay count read 236, and was 239

What was wrong. The published report count was sixteen days stale. The harvester
had been returning nothing since 2026-09-18 because the source began refusing automated
requests that day, and the guard correctly refused to overwrite the record with zero — but
the resulting staleness was not surfaced.

What changed. The harvester now uses a transport the source accepts. The corrected
figures: 239 reports, 33 below 98%, average 99.03%, floor 93.844%.

## Why this page exists

A publication that has never corrected anything is either very short-lived or not
looking. Ours is neither, so the list is here.
