## Why the chain matters more than a screenshot

A screenshot shows that something appeared on a screen once. It cannot show that the
record was not edited afterwards, and it cannot be recomputed by a reader. Each entry in
our ledger is a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry’s hash joined to that
entry’s canonical record. Changing any figure in any earlier row changes that
row’s hash, and therefore every hash after it, which is visible to anyone who
recomputes the chain. That is the difference between an image of evidence and evidence.

The canonical record and both hashes are printed for every entry, so the check needs
nothing from us beyond the page itself.

### What none of this establishes

It does not establish that the products work. A purity figure is a measure of
composition, not of effect.

- It does not establish the laboratory’s accreditation tier. We report where
a report resolves, not what the laboratory is accredited to do.

- No blind test exists. Nobody has bought this vendor’s product anonymously
and sent it for independent testing — not us, and not for any vendor in this category in
Canada.

- The cart test stops before payment. No identity, address or payment details are
entered and no order is submitted, so it says nothing about fulfilment, shipping or
returns.

- We are an affiliate. We earn a commission when a reader uses HEALTHYLIFE10. That is
a reason to check our figures rather than take them, which is why they are published in a
form that can be checked.
