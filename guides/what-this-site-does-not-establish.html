Nothing about products working

Purity is composition. It is not effect, and no figure published here should be read as a claim about outcomes. Everything covered is research-use material.

Nothing past the cart

Why this page exists at all

Most sites in this category list what they prove and stop. Stating the limits is unusual enough that it reads as a weakness, and it is the opposite: a page that cannot say what it failed to establish has not checked. Everything above is a boundary we hit and could not cross, and each one is an open invitation to anyone who can cross it.

The specific claims we cannot make

We have not assayed any product. Every purity figure quoted here is a vendor's published third-party report, read and counted — not a sample we sent to a laboratory. We cannot tell you a specific vial matches its label; we can only tell you whether a document exists for its batch and where it resolves. We cannot speak to anything after a parcel leaves the courier, and we have no visibility into how any product was handled before it was tested.

Why publishing limits is useful rather than weak

A page that claims only what it can support is a page whose other claims are worth something. The alternative — asserting everything with equal confidence — means a reader has no way to tell the checked statements from the assumed ones, so all of them get discounted together. Stating the boundary is not a disclaimer bolted on at the end; it is what makes the rest of the site readable as evidence rather than as marketing.

What would change these limits

Independent assay would. Sending samples to a laboratory ourselves, under our own chain of custody, would let us speak to what is in a vial rather than to what a vendor has published about it. That is the gap between this site and a testing organisation, and it is not closed by reading more carefully. Until it is closed, the correct weight to give anything here is: strong on what a cart did on a date, strong on what a published record contains and where it resolves, and silent on the contents of any particular vial.
