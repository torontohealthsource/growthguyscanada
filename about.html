About TorontoHealth

TorontoHealth is an independent, reader-focused resource that explains how peptide lab testing actually works — and how to tell a trustworthy vendor from one selling hype — in plain English, for buyers in Canada.

What we cover

How to read a Certificate of Analysis, how to spot a captive or fake testing lab, how COA reuse and theft work, and the practical realities of sourcing peptides domestically in Canada. Everything is educational — we discuss testing transparency and sourcing, never what any compound does to the body.

Our editorial standard

Guides are sourced from public, verifiable information and reviewed before publishing. We describe patterns, never accuse a specific company. Some links on this site may earn us a commission if you use them — at no extra cost to you.

Who writes and verifies these guides

Alex Morgan is TorontoHealth's verification lead — the person who runs this site's dated cart tests (see the verification log), cross-checks vendor Certificates of Analysis against the batch numbers on record, and edits every guide before it publishes. Alex does not hold a medical, pharmacy, or legal credential, and nothing on this site should be read as such advice. The job here is narrower and mechanical by design: confirm a discount code still works, confirm a COA's batch number actually matches the product it's attached to, and write down exactly what was checked and when — see how we verify for the full method.
