# Who publishes this, and how it is funded

Published by Toronto Health Source. Updated October 6, 2026.

## What this is

An independent Canadian site that tests discount codes against live carts and parses published third-party laboratory reports, then publishes both with dates. It is not operated by, owned by, or affiliated with any vendor it writes about.

## The byline

Articles carry an organisational byline and no personal one. We do not invent reviewers, and there is no named individual to attribute expertise to — the evidence is the credential, which is why every figure links to the record behind it.

## How we are paid

Affiliate commission when a reader uses the code, at no extra cost to them. That is a reason to check our figures rather than take them, which is exactly why every one of them is published in a form that can be checked independently.

## What is automated and what is not

The cart tests are automated and run every day — 692 reads so far. The laboratory record is parsed from the vendor's published reports. The writing is reviewed before it goes out. Nothing is published that was not measured.

## Corrections

Errors are logged with their dates on the corrections page rather than silently edited. If you find one, the fastest route is the correction route on that page.

## What we are not

Not a medical resource, not a vendor, and not a reviewer of outcomes. Everything covered is research-use material and nothing here is medical advice.
