## What TorontoHealth is

 An independent, reader-focused resource explaining how peptide lab testing works and how to
 vet a vendor, for buyers in Canada. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Growth Guys or any
 other vendor discussed on this site.

 
## Sources we use

 
 Regulatory publications — Health Canada advisories, the Food and Drugs Act, and related guidance
 
- Lab accreditation standards (ISO 17025, CLIA, and similar)
 
- Vendor-published data — store catalogs, pricing, and Certificates of Analysis
 
- Cart-level checkout observations this site runs itself, with the raw store API response retained as evidence — see the verification methodology
 

 
## What we don't publish

 
 
- Claims about what a compound does to the body — educational and sourcing framing only
 
- No disease-treatment or cure claims
 
- Fabricated first-person product experience or invented testimonials
 
- A specific company named as committing fraud — patterns are described anonymized instead, to avoid defamation risk while still being useful
 

 
## How a draft gets checked before it ships

 Every article runs through an automated compliance gate that scans for disease claims,
 unhedged benefit language, fabricated testimonials, and mental-health-adjacent wording — any
 hit routes the draft to human review instead of auto-publishing. Anything touching a
 health-adjacent claim requires a human read before it goes live, regardless of what the
 automated check says.

 
## Corrections

 If a published page is found to be materially wrong, it's corrected on the page with the
 change visible — not quietly edited out. The verification log
 keeps every past result, including failed checks, rather than only showing the current status.
