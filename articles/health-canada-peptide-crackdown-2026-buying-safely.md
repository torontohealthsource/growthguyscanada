## Are peptides legal in Canada?

Research peptides are legal to purchase and possess when properly labeled for laboratory research use. They are not approved by Health Canada for human therapeutic use, and a “research use only” label does not make human injection legal. The distinction Health Canada draws is about labeling and marketing — not the compound itself.

## How do I tell if a peptide vendor is legit?

| Signal | Lower risk | Red flag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lab testing | Independent third-party (e.g. Janoshik) | Seller's own in-house numbers only |
| Lot matching | COA lot number matches the vial received | Generic COA, no lot verification |
| Labeling | Explicit "research use only," no dosing-for-humans language | Marketed with human dosing/injection instructions |
| Claims | No therapeutic/medical claims | Promises of treatment, cure, or guaranteed results |

- Ask for the Certificate of Analysis before you buy, not after.

- Confirm the testing lab is independent — a seller grading its own homework isn't verification.

- Check that the lot number on the COA matches what's printed on your vial.

- Walk away from any listing that talks about human dosing, treatment, or medical outcomes.
