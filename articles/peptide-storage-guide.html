Peptide Storage Guide: Lyophilized vs. Reconstituted (Canada 2026)

What's the short version? Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides are commonly described as the more stable form — sealed, frozen, with desiccant. Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, the commonly described practice shifts to refrigeration and a shorter usable window. This page covers general handling conventions, not a recommendation for any specific compound or schedule.

After reconstitution

Adding bacteriostatic water changes the picture: the peptide is now in solution, which is generally described as less stable over time than the freeze-dried form. Common practice described in the space:

  • Refrigerate (roughly 2–8°C) rather than freeze once reconstituted
  • Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycling, commonly described as a stress factor
  • Keep away from direct light — many peptides are described as light-sensitive, which is why vials commonly ship in amber glass
  • Shorter usable windows are commonly described post-reconstitution than the sealed lyophilized form

Specifics vary by compound. This page describes general conventions in the space, not a guarantee or instruction for any particular peptide — if you're calculating concentration or volume, the reconstitution calculator does the arithmetic on your own numbers; it doesn't tell you what to store or how long.

FAQ

How long do lyophilized peptides last? Commonly described as 12–24 months sealed at -20°C with desiccant; faster degradation at room temperature. General convention, not a guarantee for a specific vial.

What changes once reconstituted? The peptide is in solution, generally described as less stable, commonly refrigerated rather than frozen.

Should reconstituted peptides be frozen? Freeze-thaw cycling is commonly described as a stress factor; refrigeration is the more commonly described practice, but specifics vary by compound.

Does light exposure matter? Many peptides are described as light-sensitive, hence amber vials and dark storage.

Is this dosing advice? No — storage and handling only.
