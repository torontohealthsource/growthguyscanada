## What you should do about it

Here's the part most people skip: a redirect is not permanent. Vendors typically retire them once the move has settled, often after a year or two. Your bookmark works today and may quietly stop working later, at which point an old link will simply fail with no explanation.

So take thirty seconds now. Open your bookmark, let it forward, and re-save the page from the new address. Do the same for any Growth Guys link sitting in a note or a group chat.

## Is the redirect itself safe to follow?

A forward from an address you already trusted to the vendor's new home is the ordinary mechanism of a domain move, and following it is how you confirm the genuine destination. What deserves a second look is the opposite situation: an unfamiliar link that lands you somewhere claiming to be the store. The address bar is the check that matters, and we walk through it in detail in our guide to [verifying the real Growth Guys site after the domain change](https://growthguyscanada.ca/articles/growth-guys-new-domain-growthguys-com.html).
