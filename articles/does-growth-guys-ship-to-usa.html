Why they are two stores rather than one with wider shipping

Shipping research compounds across an international border introduces a customs entry, and a customs entry introduces an authority that can inspect, delay, assess duty on, or refuse a parcel. A domestic network has none of those decision points. Running two domestic operations removes the border from every order rather than managing it on each one.

That is why the split is a feature rather than an inconvenience, and it is the same reason a Canadian buyer should prefer the Canadian store over any seller shipping in. The relevant cost is not postage; it is the probability that a parcel is held somewhere you cannot reach it.
