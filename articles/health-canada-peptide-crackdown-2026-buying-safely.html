Are peptides legal in Canada?

Research peptides are legal to purchase and possess when properly labeled for laboratory research use. They are not approved by Health Canada for human therapeutic use, and a “research use only” label does not make human injection legal. The distinction Health Canada draws is about labeling and marketing — not the compound itself.

What did Health Canada actually announce in 2026?

Public advisories on canada.ca named unauthorized injectable peptide sellers including a company called Canada Peptide, an advisory tied to a Calgary wellness centre, action against a seller called Prime Research, and a permanent injunction against a company called Canlab Research. Coverage from CBC News and Global News confirmed the injunction, and the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta separately warned members about unauthorized injectable peptides reaching patients.
