Why a domain change is the moment to slow down

This is the part worth your attention, and it has nothing to do with Growth Guys specifically. Any time a known store changes its web address, that switch becomes the single best opening for lookalike sites. The pattern is well documented across e-commerce generally: someone registers a near-identical spelling, copies the real storefront's design wholesale, buys a little search traffic, and collects payment details from people who were already expecting the address to look slightly different than they remembered.

The move itself is not the risk. The risk is that a genuine change trains you to accept an unfamiliar address without checking — and that habit is what a clone counts on.
