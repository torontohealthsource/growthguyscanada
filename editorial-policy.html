Editorial Policy

The short version: public, verifiable sources only; no claims about what a compound does to the body; no fabricated experience; every draft passes an automated compliance check before it ships; corrections happen visibly, not silently.

What TorontoHealth is

An independent, reader-focused resource explaining how peptide lab testing works and how to vet a vendor, for buyers in Canada. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Growth Guys or any other vendor discussed on this site.

Sources we use

  • Regulatory publications — Health Canada advisories, the Food and Drugs Act, and related guidance
  • Lab accreditation standards (ISO 17025, CLIA, and similar)
  • Vendor-published data — store catalogs, pricing, and Certificates of Analysis
  • Cart-level checkout observations this site runs itself, with the raw store API response retained as evidence — see the verification methodology

What we don't publish

  • Claims about what a compound does to the body — educational and sourcing framing only
  • No disease-treatment or cure claims
  • Fabricated first-person product experience or invented testimonials
  • A specific company named as committing fraud — patterns are described anonymized instead, to avoid defamation risk while still being useful

How a draft gets checked before it ships

Every article runs through an automated compliance gate that scans for disease claims, unhedged benefit language, fabricated testimonials, and mental-health-adjacent wording — any hit routes the draft to human review instead of auto-publishing. Anything touching a health-adjacent claim requires a human read before it goes live, regardless of what the automated check says.

Corrections

If a published page is found to be materially wrong, it's corrected on the page with the change visible — not quietly edited out. The verification log keeps every past result, including failed checks, rather than only showing the current status.

The commercial relationship, plainly

This site promotes specific Growth Guys discount codes and may earn a commission when they're used. That relationship doesn't change what gets published — the sourcing standards and compliance gate above apply regardless of the commercial angle of a given page.

FAQ

What is TorontoHealth? An independent resource on peptide testing transparency and sourcing, not affiliated with or endorsed by any vendor discussed.

What sources are used? Public regulatory publications, lab accreditation standards, vendor-published data, and this site's own cart-level checkout observations.

What claims are avoided? Body-effect claims, disease-treatment claims, fabricated testimonials, and naming a company as fraudulent.

How are corrections handled? Visibly, on the page — never silently edited.

Is there a financial relationship with vendors discussed? Yes, via discount-code commissions — disclosed here, and it doesn't change editorial decisions.
