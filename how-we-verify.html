How We Verify Growth Guys Codes

The short version: every code on this site is tested against a real guest cart on growthguys.com's own store API, daily, automated, with the raw cart response retained as evidence — not a screenshot, not a claim. Passes and failures are both published, dated, in the public log.

What exactly gets tested

1. A fresh guest cart is created. No account, no saved payment method, no shipping address — just the store's public cart API.
2. A real product is added. The item rotates — cheap, mid, and high-value listings across the week — so the result isn't dependent on one product category.
3. One code is applied. HEALTHYLIFE10.
4. The discount is checked. A PASS requires the discount to equal exactly 10% of the subtotal at that timestamp.
5. The raw response is retained. Not a rendered screenshot — the store's actual cart API JSON, linked from the log so anyone can inspect it.

No order is ever submitted and no payment method is ever entered at any step. This is an observation of the cart stage only.

What a PASS means — and what it doesn't

It means: the code applied and produced exactly a 10% discount, in one specific cart, at one specific timestamp. That's it.

It doesn't mean: a guarantee for your specific cart, a guarantee for a future date, anything about product quality, shipping, or the vendor beyond the discount mechanics itself. Storefront terms can change without notice — that's exactly why this is re-checked daily instead of listed once.

What happens when a check fails

It gets published as a failure, dated, in the same log as every pass — nothing has ever been removed or hidden. Coverage that only shows successes isn't verification, it's marketing.

Corrections policy

If a published check or article turns out to be materially wrong, it's corrected on the page with the change visible, not silently edited out. Method-first accuracy matters more than a clean-looking track record.

What this is not

TorontoHealth is an independent research resource, not Growth Guys. This page describes our own checks — it isn't an official statement from the vendor, and their live checkout and published terms remain the final authority on any order.

FAQ

What exactly gets tested? A fresh guest cart, a real rotating product, one code applied, checked against the expected 10% — no account, payment, or order ever used.

How often is this run? Daily, automated. Every run is published, pass or fail.

What counts as evidence here versus a badge? The store's raw cart API response, retained and linked from every log entry — not a rendered claim.

Is this an official Growth Guys statement? No — independent research resource, own methodology, own checks.
