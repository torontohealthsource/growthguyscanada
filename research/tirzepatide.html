The published record for this compound

Tirzepatide appears in 8 of the 239 third-party reports this vendor publishes, across 6 distinct batch identifiers. The 8 results run from 99.19% to 99.88%, a spread of 0.69 points. A spread is not a fault; it is what separate batches actually look like, and a set with no spread at all would be the thing worth questioning.

Labelled strength against measured mass

7 of the Tirzepatide reports state a measured mass against a labelled strength. 7 measured at or above the label and 0 below it. This is a separate question from purity and the one most readers skip: a vial can be highly pure and still contain less than the label claims, because purity describes the fraction of what is there and mass describes how much of it there is.

What the record shows batch by batch

The strongest result on record for Tirzepatide is batch TZ06 at 99.88%, and the weakest is TZ03 at 99.19%, a gap of 0.69 points across 6 batches. Both are published, which is the part worth noticing: a seller choosing what to show could have shown only the first. 2 batch identifier(s) appear more than once in the set, so the same batch has been tested on separate occasions rather than once and filed. Reading down the Purity column rather than at a headline average is the whole method here — an average hides its own floor.

Matching a batch

The batch identifiers published for Tirzepatide are TZ03, TZ04, TZ04-10, TZ05, TZ06, TZ07. Match the one on the vial against that list. A report is evidence about one batch — not a product line, not a vendor — so a batch absent from the table above is a batch this record says nothing about.
