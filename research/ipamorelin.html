The assay record for Ipamorelin, read off this vendor's published reports. Updated October 5, 2026.

The published record for this compound

Ipamorelin appears in 7 of the 239 third-party reports this vendor publishes, across 7 distinct batch identifiers. The 4 results sit in a band 0.07 percentage points wide, from 99.82% to 99.89%. A tight band across separate batches is the more interesting observation here — it says the process repeated, which a single high number cannot.

Labelled strength against measured mass

4 of the Ipamorelin reports state a measured mass against a labelled strength. 4 measured at or above the label and 0 below it. This is a separate question from purity and the one most readers skip: a vial can be highly pure and still contain less than the label claims, because purity describes the fraction of what is there and mass describes how much of it there is.

What the record shows batch by batch

The strongest result on record for Ipamorelin is batch IPA02 at 99.89%, and the weakest is IPA03 at 99.82%, a gap of 0.07 points across 7 batches. Both are published, which is the part worth noticing: a seller choosing what to show could have shown only the first. Reading down the Purity column rather than at a headline average is the whole method here — an average hides its own floor.

Matching a batch

The batch identifiers published for Ipamorelin are CJCIPA01, CJCIPA03, CJCIPA04, IPA01, IPA02, IPA03, IPA04. Match the one on the vial against that list. A report is evidence about one batch — not a product line, not a vendor — so a batch absent from the table above is a batch this record says nothing about.
