TB-500: what the published lab record actually shows

The assay record for TB-500, read off this vendor's published reports. Updated October 5, 2026.

The published record for this compound

TB-500 appears in 18 of the 239 third-party reports this vendor publishes, across 18 distinct batch identifiers. The 8 results run from 97.66% to 99.87%, a spread of 2.21 points. A spread is not a fault; it is what separate batches actually look like, and a set with no spread at all would be the thing worth questioning. One of them falls below 98%, published alongside the rest rather than dropped.

Labelled strength against measured mass

14 of the TB-500 reports state a measured mass against a labelled strength. 13 measured at or above the label and 1 below it, the lowest at 99.5% of its labelled strength. This is a separate question from purity and the one most readers skip: a vial can be highly pure and still contain less than the label claims, because purity describes the fraction of what is there and mass describes how much of it there is.

What the record shows batch by batch

The strongest result on record for TB-500 is batch TB08 at 99.87%, and the weakest is TB09 at 97.66%, a gap of 2.21 points across 18 batches. Both are published, which is the part worth noticing: a seller choosing what to show could have shown only the first. Reading down the Purity column rather than at a headline average is the whole method here — an average hides its own floor.

Matching a batch

The batch identifiers published for TB-500 are BPCTB01, BPCTB02, BPCTB03, BPCTB04, BPCTB05, BPCTB06, BPCTB07, BPCTB08 and 10 more. Match the one on the vial against that list. A report is evidence about one batch — not a product line, not a vendor — so a batch absent from the table above is a batch this record says nothing about.
