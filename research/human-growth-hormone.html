The published record for this compound

Human Growth Hormone appears in 20 of the 239 third-party reports this vendor publishes, across 20 distinct batch identifiers. The 20 results run from 93.844% to 97.85%, a spread of 4.01 points. A spread is not a fault; it is what separate batches actually look like, and a set with no spread at all would be the thing worth questioning. 20 of them fall below 98%, published alongside the rest rather than dropped.

Labelled strength against measured mass

Too few reports for this compound state a measured mass to say anything useful about labelled versus measured strength. Purity and mass answer different questions, and only one of them is well covered here.

What the record shows batch by batch

The strongest result on record for Human Growth Hormone is batch GGC36 at 97.85%, and the weakest is GGC38 at 93.844%, a gap of 4.01 points across 20 batches. Both are published, which is the part worth noticing: a seller choosing what to show could have shown only the first. Reading down the Purity column rather than at a headline average is the whole method here — an average hides its own floor.

Matching a batch

The batch identifiers published for Human Growth Hormone are GGC16, GGC17, GGC18, GGC19, GGC20, GGC21, GGC22, GGC23 and 12 more. Match the one on the vial against that list. A report is evidence about one batch — not a product line, not a vendor — so a batch absent from the table above is a batch this record says nothing about.
