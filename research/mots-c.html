The published record for this compound

MOTS-C appears in 7 of the 239 third-party reports this vendor publishes, across 7 distinct batch identifiers. The 7 results run from 98.84% to 99.84%, a spread of 1.00 points. Labelled strength against measured mass

5 of the MOTS-C reports state a measured mass against a labelled strength. 5 measured at or above the label and 0 below it. This is a separate question from purity and the one most readers skip: a vial can be highly pure and still contain less than the label claims, because purity describes the fraction of what is there and mass describes how much of it there is.

What the record shows batch by batch

The strongest result on record for MOTS-C is batch MOTS06 at 99.84%, and the weakest is MOTS08 at 98.84%, a gap of 1.00 points across 7 batches. Both are published, which is the part worth noticing: a seller choosing what to show could have shown only the first. Reading down the Purity column rather than at a headline average is the whole method here — an average hides its own floor.

Matching a batch

The batch identifiers published for MOTS-C are MOTS01, MOTS02, MOTS04, MOTS05, MOTS06, MOTS07, MOTS08. Match the one on the vial against that list. A report is evidence about one batch — not a product line, not a vendor — so a batch absent from the table above is a batch this record says nothing about.
